terms

How to order, pay and receive. If your question isn't answered here, message me on Telegram.

How to place an order
  1. Fill your cart in the shop.
  2. Choose the delivery country, the cost is calculated automatically.
  3. Fill in the recipient details and copy the order template.
  4. Send the template to me on Telegram.
  5. I'll reply, confirm availability and the final total, and send payment details.

The site doesn't send anything automatically: an order is placed only by messaging me.

Payment

Payment is by bank transfer after I confirm the order. The order is reserved for 3 days; if there is no payment by then, it is cancelled.

Shipping and delivery times

I ship within 5 business days after payment. The cost is a base rate plus a surcharge for every 100 g of order weight. The cart calculates it automatically, and I confirm the final amount in my reply.

DestinationTimeBase ratePer 100 gFree shipping

Delivery times are approximate and depend on the postal service. I'll send the tracking number right after shipping.

If something goes wrong

If an item arrives defective or isn't what you ordered, message me within 7 days and attach an unboxing photo. I'll replace the item or refund you. If a parcel is lost in transit, we'll sort it out together with the postal service.

Custom merch

The minimum quantity and the price depend on the product. After your request I send a mockup and a quote. Production starts after a 50% prepayment, the rest is paid before shipping. The mockup includes up to 2 rounds of changes. Custom orders can't be refunded once production has started.

Digital commissions

Payment is split: 50% before I start, the rest after the sketch is approved and before I send the final files. The price includes 2 rounds of small changes on the sketch. Commissions are for personal use unless we agree otherwise, commercial use costs extra. I may decline requests I'm not comfortable drawing, and I may show finished work in my portfolio.

Copyright

All drawings are mine. Fandom works are unofficial fan art. Reselling the merch or using my art without an agreement is not allowed.
